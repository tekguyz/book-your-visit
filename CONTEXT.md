# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Book Your Visit | The product: a booking page where customers pick a time themselves, and the owner's view of each day. TEKGUYZ Custom Web Apps, offering 4. |
| Sample Business | Tidy Heron Home Cleaning, the made-up cleaning company the demo plays. Not a real business. |
| Customer | The person booking. In the demo, the visitor plays Dana K. |
| Owner | The person who runs the business and its day. In the demo, Yolanda. |
| Service | What the customer books. Each one has a length: Standard clean, Deep clean, Move-in or move-out clean. |
| Open time | A start time the booking page offers: inside opening hours, a travel buffer from every other visit, and no blocked time. |
| Travel buffer | 30 minutes kept free between two visits, to drive between homes. |
| Block | Time the owner takes off the booking page. It can never cover a booked visit. |
| Schedule | Every visit, block and drafted message in one demo (`lib/demo/engine.ts`). |
| What would go out | The list of every email and text the system would send. In the demo, none is sent. |
