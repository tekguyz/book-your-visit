# Product

**Who it is for.** Owners of small home-service businesses in South Florida who still book by phone and text: cleaners, lawn crews, window washers, handymen. Every call that interrupts a job is a booking that may not happen.

**The problem.** Customers want to pick a time at night or on a phone, not wait for a callback. The owner juggles a notebook, texts and a calendar, and double books.

**The fix (TEKGUYZ Custom Web Apps, "Appointment and scheduling portals").** A booking page where customers choose a service, see only the times a crew can really take, book, and move or cancel the visit themselves. The owner sees the day and blocks off time.

**This repo** is the landing page and the one-click demo that shows that fix end to end, with made-up data. It is not a product a business logs into. A real setup is built around that business's own services, crews and calendar.

**Voice.** Plain, warm, honest. No prices, no statistics, no setup-time claims, no phone numbers. Never say it is already running for anyone.
