# Product

**Who it is for.** Owners of small home-service and appointment businesses in South Florida: cleaners, handymen, detailers, mobile groomers. Today a customer calls or texts to ask for a time, and the owner answers between jobs.

**The problem.** Setting a time takes a back-and-forth. Customers who want to book after hours wait or go elsewhere. Moving or canceling a visit means another call, and the owner keeps the day in their head or on paper.

**The fix (TEKGUYZ Custom Web Apps, offering 4).** A booking page in the business's name. The customer picks a service, sees only the times the business can really make (inside opening hours, with room to drive between homes, never over blocked time), and books in a minute. They get a confirmation with a link to change or cancel. The owner sees each day with every visit and the free gaps, blocks off time in two taps, and gets a text for every booking, move or cancel.

**This repo** is the landing page and the one-click demo that shows that fix end to end, with made-up data. It is not a product a business logs into. A real portal is built for the business, its services and its calendar.

**Voice.** Plain, calm, honest. No prices, no statistics, no setup-time claims, no phone numbers. Never say it is already running for anyone.
